Privacy Policy

Last updated — June 2026

Overview

KODA ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used when you use the KODA device, companion app, or website.

By using any KODA product, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Audio recordings captured by your KODA device during active recording sessions
  • Transcripts and AI-generated summaries derived from your recordings
  • Account information such as name, email address, and password
  • Device identifiers and firmware version of your KODA hardware
  • Usage data including session frequency, feature interactions, and app crash reports
  • Calendar metadata if you choose to connect a calendar integration

How We Use Your Data

  • To transcribe, summarize, and extract action items from your meeting recordings
  • To sync your data across your devices and integrations (Slack, Notion, Jira, etc.)
  • To improve our AI transcription accuracy and product features
  • To send transactional emails such as account verification and order confirmations
  • To diagnose hardware and software issues and provide support
  • To comply with legal obligations where required

Data Storage & Security

  • All recordings and transcripts are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Your KODA device stores recordings locally on 32GB flash storage until synced
  • We do not sell your personal data or recordings to third parties
  • AI processing may be performed on secure third-party infrastructure under strict data processing agreements

Data Retention

  • Recordings and transcripts are retained for as long as your account is active
  • You may delete individual recordings or your entire account at any time from the app
  • Upon account deletion, your data is permanently purged within 30 days
  • Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics may be retained indefinitely

Third-Party Integrations

  • Connecting Slack, Notion, Jira, or Calendar shares only the data necessary for that integration
  • Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies
  • You can disconnect any integration at any time from the app settings

Your Rights

  • Access a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Children's Privacy

KODA is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify you via email if changes are material. Continued use of KODA after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, reach us at privacy@koda.ai
